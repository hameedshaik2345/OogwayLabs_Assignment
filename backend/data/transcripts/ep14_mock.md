# Lenny's Podcast: Episode 14 - Growth Levers with Jane Doe

Lenny: Welcome to the podcast! Today we have Jane Doe, VP of Product. Jane, what's your top advice for early-stage growth?

Jane: Thanks, Lenny. Early stage growth is all about retention before acquisition. A classic mistake is pouring money into ads when your product doesn't stick. The first lever is always "Activation" - making sure users hit the aha moment within the first 5 minutes. 

Lenny: And how do you measure activation?

Jane: We track it using a metric we call "Time to First Value" (TTFV). If TTFV is high, your onboarding is broken. Fix the onboarding by removing friction. Don't ask for a credit card upfront if you don't have to.

Lenny: What about Ship 30 for 30? How does writing help product managers?

Jane: Writing clarifies thinking. If you can't write a 1-pager on the feature, you shouldn't build it. Writing every day builds a muscle for concise communication, which is the most underrated product skill.

Lenny: That's amazing. Thanks, Jane!
